Designing Change Together

Connecting students with nonprofit organizations through real-world design projects, professional experiences, and community partnerships.

Project Overview

Designing Change Together was a community-focused initiative I developed and led to connect students with nonprofit organizations through meaningful, real-world design projects.

Students had the opportunity to work with actual organizations to design and build websites that addressed their needs. These projects allowed students to apply classroom knowledge, work with clients, and create professional portfolio pieces they could use when pursuing employment.

The experience also incorporated professional preparation. Students were required to submit resumes and participate in interviews, giving them an opportunity to practice skills they would need when entering the design industry.

The Challenge

Students often complete academic assignments without experiencing the expectations of working with an actual client.

I wanted to provide opportunities for students to develop more than technical design skills. They also needed experience preparing resumes, participating in interviews, communicating professionally, understanding client needs, and producing work they could confidently discuss with future employers.

At the same time, nonprofit organizations could benefit from student design talent and support with their digital presence.

My Leadership Role

I developed and led Designing Change Together as an initiative that connected curriculum, community engagement, and career preparation.

  • Established the initiative and its educational purpose.
  • Connected students with nonprofit organizations through authentic design opportunities.
  • Integrated professional resume preparation and interview practice into the student experience.
  • Connected client-based projects with curriculum learning objectives and practical skill development.
  • Supported opportunities for students to experience professional client expectations and collaboration.
  • Emphasized the development of completed projects that students could include in their portfolios.

From Interview to Finished Project

The initiative gave students experience with several important stages of professional work, beginning with career preparation and continuing through the completion of real-world design projects.

01 / RESUME PREPARATION

Preparing for Opportunities

Students prepared and submitted resumes, gaining practical experience presenting their skills, education, and qualifications for a professional opportunity.

02 / INTERVIEW EXPERIENCE

Practicing Professional Interviews

Students participated in interviews, allowing them to practice communicating their strengths, discussing their experience, and presenting themselves in a professional setting.

03 / REAL-WORLD CLIENT PROJECT

Applying Design Skills

Students worked on website design and development projects for nonprofit organizations, gaining experience creating work for real clients rather than hypothetical assignments.

04 / PROFESSIONAL PORTFOLIO

Showcasing Completed Work

Students completed websites that could be featured in their professional portfolios, giving them concrete examples of their abilities to share with potential employers.
