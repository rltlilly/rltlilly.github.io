Program Management & Leadership

Designing Change Together

Connecting students with nonprofit organizations through authentic design projects and community-based learning.

Project Overview

Designing Change Together was an initiative that connected design students with nonprofit organizations to work on real-world projects.

The initiative gave students opportunities to apply their skills beyond classroom assignments while creating work they could include in their professional portfolios.

The Challenge

Students needed opportunities to apply their learning to realistic client expectations, collaborate with others, and create meaningful work that demonstrated career readiness.

My Role & Approach

  • Developed and led the initiative.
  • Connected coursework with community-based projects.
  • Supported collaboration with nonprofit partners.
  • Aligned project experiences with learning objectives.
  • Encouraged practical application of design skills.
  • Helped connect project outcomes to student portfolios.

Results & Impact

Students gained experience applying design skills to authentic projects while creating portfolio artifacts and contributing to nonprofit initiatives.

The initiative connected curriculum expectations with practical experience and community engagement.
