01 / Project Overview

Connecting learning with real-world opportunity.

Designing Change Together was a community-focused initiative I developed and led to connect students with nonprofit organizations through meaningful design projects.

Students had the opportunity to work with actual organizations to design and build websites that addressed their needs. These projects allowed students to apply classroom knowledge, collaborate with clients, and create professional portfolio pieces.

The experience also included professional preparation. Students submitted resumes and participated in interviews, giving them opportunities to practice skills they would need when entering the design industry.

The goal was to help students build more than technical skills. I wanted them to gain experience they could carry into their careers.
02 / The Challenge

Moving beyond classroom assignments.

Students often complete academic assignments without experiencing what it means to work with an actual client. I wanted to create an opportunity that connected technical learning, professional preparation, and community needs.

01 / PROFESSIONAL SKILLS

Career Preparation

Students needed opportunities to practice resume preparation, interviewing, and communicating professionally.

02 / PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE

Authentic Projects

Students needed experience understanding real client expectations and applying design skills beyond hypothetical assignments.

03 / COMMUNITY IMPACT

Nonprofit Support

Nonprofit organizations could benefit from student design talent and assistance strengthening their online presence.
