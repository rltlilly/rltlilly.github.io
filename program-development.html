Building & Launching Four Online Programs

Leading programs from initial concept and strategic planning through curriculum development, implementation, launch, and ongoing operations.

Project Overview

I led the end-to-end development and launch of four online degree and certificate programs in UX/UI Design and Graphic Design. My responsibilities spanned the full program lifecycle, from early planning and curriculum architecture to course development, faculty recruitment, implementation, launch, and ongoing program management.

I coordinated cross-functional teams, established instructional and assessment standards, and developed processes to support consistent program delivery. Following launch, I continued to oversee program operations, evaluate performance, and lead improvements informed by data, faculty feedback, and industry expectations.

The Challenge

Building new academic programs required coordinating multiple moving parts while maintaining academic quality and meeting implementation goals.

Each program needed a clear curriculum structure, aligned learning outcomes, meaningful assessments, qualified faculty, and consistent online course experiences. At the same time, the programs needed to reflect current industry expectations and prepare students with practical, career-relevant skills.

My responsibility was to bring these components together, manage development priorities, coordinate the people involved, and move each program successfully from planning into operation.

My Leadership Role

As Program Director, I was responsible for the development, implementation, and ongoing management of the programs. My role combined strategic leadership with hands-on knowledge of curriculum development and learning technology.

  • Strategic Planning: Led program planning, established development priorities, and coordinated implementation activities.
  • Curriculum Leadership: Directed program structures, course sequencing, learning outcomes, and assessment alignment.
  • Faculty Leadership: Recruited, onboarded, supervised, and supported faculty responsible for course delivery and student learning.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Worked with academic leadership, instructional design, technology, and student support teams to coordinate development and launch activities.
  • Launch Management: Coordinated course readiness, faculty assignments, scheduling, and operational requirements for implementation.
  • Program Operations: Oversaw ongoing delivery, faculty performance, student progress, and program quality.
  • Continuous Improvement: Used learning analytics, program reviews, assessment findings, and industry feedback to guide curriculum and process improvements.

Program Quality & Continuous Improvement

My responsibilities continued beyond launching the programs. I led ongoing reviews and improvements to maintain academic quality, instructional consistency, and alignment with industry expectations.

  • Developed and reviewed Program Learning Outcomes Assessment Plans (PLOAPs), including measures, benchmarks, and improvement actions.
  • Led programmatic reviews to evaluate curriculum effectiveness and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Worked with the Program Advisory Committee to incorporate employer and industry feedback.
  • Directed curriculum improvements emphasizing project-based learning, authentic assessments, and professional portfolio development.
  • Used Canvas analytics and Power BI to monitor performance and support data-informed decisions.
  • Supported faculty development, instructional consistency, accessibility, and course quality.
