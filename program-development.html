01 / Project Overview

Leading the full program lifecycle.

I led the end-to-end development and launch of four online degree and certificate programs in UX/UI Design and Graphic Design. My responsibilities spanned the full program lifecycle, from early planning and curriculum architecture to course development, faculty recruitment, implementation, launch, and ongoing program management.

I coordinated cross-functional teams, established instructional and assessment standards, and developed processes to support consistent program delivery. Following launch, I continued to oversee operations, evaluate performance, and lead improvements informed by data, faculty feedback, and industry expectations.

My role combined strategic leadership with hands-on curriculum expertise and operational oversight.

03 / The Challenge

Building programs that were ready to deliver.

Building new academic programs required coordinating multiple moving parts while maintaining academic quality and meeting implementation goals.

Each program needed a clear curriculum structure, aligned learning outcomes, meaningful assessments, qualified faculty, and consistent online course experiences. The programs also needed to reflect industry expectations and prepare students with practical, career-relevant skills.

My responsibility was to bring these components together, manage development priorities, coordinate the teams involved, and move each program from planning into operation.
