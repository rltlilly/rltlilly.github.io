Curriculum & Program Development

Online Program Development & Redesign

Leading the development, launch, and improvement of online programs aligned with learner needs and industry expectations.

Project Overview

I led the development, launch, and ongoing management of three online programs in UX/UI Design, Graphic Design, and Web Design.

My work covered the program lifecycle, including curriculum planning, course development, implementation, program reviews, and continuous improvement.

The Challenge

Developing online programs required more than creating courses. The curriculum needed to support clear learning outcomes, practical application, consistent instruction, and preparation for industry expectations.

My Role & Approach

  • Led curriculum development and course sequencing.
  • Aligned assessments with program learning outcomes.
  • Directed curriculum reviews and redesign initiatives.
  • Worked with faculty and instructional support teams.
  • Used Program Advisory Committee feedback to guide improvements.
  • Supported consistent course quality and accessibility.
  • Oversaw implementation and ongoing program operations.

Results & Impact

Led the launch and management of three online programs, including initial launches completed within approximately five months.

Curriculum improvements emphasized project-based learning, authentic assessments, and portfolio development to strengthen career readiness.
