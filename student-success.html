Program Management & Leadership

Student Success & Process Improvement

Using learning analytics, early intervention, and cross-functional collaboration to improve student support and program outcomes.

Project Overview

As a Program Director, I monitored student engagement, academic progress, and retention to identify challenges and improve support processes across online programs.

The Challenge

Online learners sometimes faced barriers related to academic progress, technology, and engagement. Addressing these challenges required early identification, coordinated support, and consistent follow-through.

My Role & Approach

  • Reviewed LMS analytics and student performance data.
  • Used Canvas and Power BI to identify trends.
  • Worked with coaches and tutors to support at-risk students.
  • Coordinated interventions and monitored student progress.
  • Improved communication between academic and support teams.
  • Used findings to improve program processes.

Results & Impact

Approximately 85% retention: Supported ongoing retention through proactive monitoring and intervention.

Below 3% SAP: Academic support and coaching processes helped maintain the proportion of students experiencing Satisfactory Academic Progress difficulties below 3% of enrollment.

Tools & Technology

Canvas LMS, Power BI, academic reporting, and cross-functional student support workflows.
